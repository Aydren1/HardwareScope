# HardwareScope 2.0.9

## New

- Reset the Current-session Minimum and Maximum columns manually from Settings or the tray menu.
- Optionally reset Min/Max when HardwareScope starts, when a game launches, or every 15 minutes through one week.
- Preview the OSD directly on the OSD settings tab without launching a game.
- Drag selected hardware sensors into the exact order you want. FPS remains fixed first for fast readability.

## Improved

- The preview reflects vertical or horizontal layout, corner placement, independent FPS placement, spacing, scale, opacity, colors, background, separators, and graph visibility.
- OSD ordering and Min/Max automation are included in settings import/export.
- Existing version 2 settings migrate automatically.
