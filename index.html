Good to know

Before you install

Why does it request administrator access?

Low-level CPU and motherboard sensors require elevated access. HardwareScope reads telemetry only and does not change fan curves, voltages, clocks, or power limits.

Why might some sensors be missing?

Coverage depends on what your hardware, firmware, and drivers expose. Unsupported devices cannot provide readings even when the app is elevated.

Does it install another runtime?

No separate runtime download is needed. The self-contained installer includes everything required to run HardwareScope.
